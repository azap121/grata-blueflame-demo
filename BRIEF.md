## The argument in one line

One product called Grata, with Blueflame's agentic muscle underneath: an **overall layer** where you ask the market anything and see what needs you, and a **Project layer** where Merlin runs governed work on one deal spine — every output cited, every commit gated, every run held somewhere you can find it.

## Demo script (~8 minutes)

### Beat 1 — The two-zone home
Open the root URL. Zone 1: **"Ask Grata anything"** — the intelligence composer, scope chip on `Universe`. Zone 2, no scroll: **Needs you** (the CIM plan gate), **Overnight** (finished runs, a fired seller-intent signal), **Projects** on the gradient (Watchlist → Live Deal). *One screen answers "what's out there" and "where was I."*

### Beat 2 — Source & screen → promote to Project
Click **"Find HVAC companies in Texas, $10M–$50M revenue."** Grata-style AI parse (editable term chips) → results table with seller-intent. Select the top three → **Promote to Deal** → *Project Caldera* lands on the home carrying the search, targets, and signals. **The re-keying cliff, killed on screen.**

### Beat 3 — The Project space
Open Project Caldera. Chat-first with Merlin; the context rail reads **Overview · Documents · Tables · Intelligence · Activity**; the Overview canvas answers "where was I" (carried chips, targets, next steps, activity). The rail's Agents library is grouped by lifecycle (Source · Evaluate · Diligence · Monitor).

### Beat 4 — `/` — the composer grammar
Type **`/`** in the composer. The instrument menu opens: **recommended playbooks first** (cim-screen "because a CIM landed in Documents"), then the stage-grouped catalog, each row carrying its **credit estimate**, footer → the full library. The grammar: **`/` picks the verb, `@` picks the material, the dial sets the trust level.** Select `cim-screen`.

### Beat 5 — The run: Merlin mode, glass box, gates
The composer flips to **Merlin** (gradient border = the "am I about to commit/spend?" glance test), the model chip becomes the **autonomy dial** (`Plan first`), the estimate reads *cim-screen · est. 14 credits · hard stop at 50*. Press Enter: glass-box work log → plan card → **Approve plan** → execution log with a visible **@Grata call** mid-run → the **cited review table** slides in (per-cell citations; provenance vocabulary `[SOURCE] [DERIVED] [ASSUMPTION] [UNVERIFIED]`; one row flagged red — concentration 38% vs thesis cap 30%) → **Accept & track** → the Overview echoes the commit. Audit stamp: *"Ran in Plan first · plan approved by you."*

### Beat 6 — Runs: the missing home
Global rail → **Runs**. Four sections: **Needs you** (the highest-value screen — everything blocked on you, across projects), Running (budget consumed live), Scheduled (*Morning Meeting Prep 07:55*, seller-intent watch), Recent (audit stamps). Open a row → the step trace drawer. *This is what turns a chatbot into an operating system.*

### Beat 7 — Create: the two authoring doors
On the accepted output card: **Save as Playbook** — the run's steps and inputs become the draft (the capture door). Then the global **+ Create** → Project · Playbook · **Agent** — the agent flow is a *contract*: capabilities with an explicit denial row (*"Financials — not granted · staged disclosure"*), approval default, budget cap. Choosing **Firm** scope reveals the review gate.

### Beat 8 — Coda: the seat toggle
**Seat** (Alex → Morgan) flips the same Project chat-first ⇄ structure-first. One object, seat-aware defaults — never a fork.

## What's real vs scripted

Everything is a timed, scripted state machine — no live agents, no real data, fictional companies. The playbook catalog (~18 entries), model roster, filter/table anatomy, and provenance vocabulary are drawn verbatim from the July 2026 platform recons. The fixtures ARE the contract a real runtime satisfies later.
